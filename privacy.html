Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What this app stores

The name, username, password, notes and reminders you enter are saved in your own browser using local storage. This app does not send them to a server, and we cannot see them.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No cookies set by the app.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.

Hosting logs

Whoever hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and requested pages, as part of serving the files.

Your choices

Log out at any time. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings permanently deletes your account, notes and reminders. Data does not sync between devices or browsers.

Security

Because data lives in your browser, anyone with access to your device and browser profile may be able to read it. Do not store highly sensitive information here.
